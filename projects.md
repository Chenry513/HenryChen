## readmeify
**Full-Stack Web App** · Feb 2026

## CardioScan
**Data Science and ML** · Dec 2025

A clinical web app that screens patients for cardiac disease risk using machine learning. Built on all four UCI Heart Disease datasets combined into a 920-patient cohort. Clinicians can enter patient measurements and get an instant risk score with a breakdown of which factors are driving it.

[Live site](https://cardiac-assessment.onrender.com) · [More detail](https://chenry513.github.io/projects/cardioscan/)
